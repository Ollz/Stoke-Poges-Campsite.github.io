Stoke Poges Campsite

Simple wild camping break in Buckinghamshire

Please Note: This site has no on-site shower facilities. Portable toilets and drinking water taps are available.

About the Campsite

[Stoke Poges Campsite](https://www.pitchup.com/campsites/England/Central/Buckinghamshire/stoke-poges/stoke-poges-campsite/) offers a basic, wild camping experience with pitch-where-you-like freedom in either woodland or open fields. It provides an easy rural getaway within walking distance of local village amenities and excellent transport links to London.

  • Location: Gerrards Cross Road, Stoke Poges, Buckinghamshire, SL2 4EU
  • Pitch Types: Non-electric grass motorhome pitches & tent/trailer tent pitches
  • Pricing: From £25 per night (2 adults, up to 4 people max per pitch)
  • Dog Policy: Dog-friendly (must be kept on lead); on-site doggie daycare available (Mon–Fri)

On-Site Facilities

  • Portable toilets & flushing toilet
  • Drinking water taps
  • Rubbish bins & recycling facilities
  • Campfires & barbecues allowed (weather permitting)

Key Site Rules & Information

  • Check-in: 10:30 AM – 8:00 PM
  • Reception: No physical reception on-site; contact the host upon arrival via the number on your booking confirmation.
  • Age Restriction: At least one guest per party must be 21 or older.

Nearby Attractions

  • Black Park Country Park (5 mins drive): Treetop courses (Go Ape) and lakeside walking/biking trails.
  • Gerrards Cross Station (5 mins drive): Direct trains reaching central London in under 30 minutes.
  • Colne Valley Regional Park (15 mins drive): Canoeing, fishing, and horse riding.
  • Cliveden Estate & Windsor Castle: Located within a 15- to 30-minute drive.
  • Local Amenities: Co-op food supermarket and local pub within walking distance.
